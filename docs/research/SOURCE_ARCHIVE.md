# Primary electoral source archive

Unmodified election statistical reports acquired from the Chief Electoral Officer, Manipur on 19 October 2026. SHA256SUMS.txt records the exact downloaded bytes. These are source publications, not the atlas's cleaned datasets. Original publication rights remain with their publishers.

- Assembly 2012: https://ceomanipur.nic.in/StatisticalReport/AssemblyElection/statistical_report_2012.pdf
- Assembly 2017: https://ceomanipur.nic.in/StatisticalReport/AssemblyElection/statistical_report_2017.pdf
- Assembly 2022: https://ceomanipur.nic.in/StatisticalReport/AssemblyElection/Statistical%20Report%202022.pdf
- Lok Sabha 2024: https://ceomanipur.nic.in/StatisticalReport/LokSabhaElection/SR_lok_Sabha_2024.pdf

The research repository preserves extracted text, reviewed transcriptions, provenance and limitations. OCR errors must not be treated as source facts. Candidate affidavits are linked to the official archive; their personal identifiers are excluded from structured exports.
